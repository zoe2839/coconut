Coconut Biochar Filter

Made from agricultural waste, our biochar adsorbs excess CO₂ and unburnt hydrocarbons before they pollute the air.

  • 100% biodegradable materials
  • High adsorption capacity
  • Made from coconut shells

Smart Sensors
